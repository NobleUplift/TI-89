(m,i)
Func
© m: confusion matrix, rows actual, columns predicted
© i: class to report, one-vs-rest
© tp,fn,fp,tn: counts for class i, returned
© n: total of every cell
© j,k: loop indices
Local tp,fn,fp,tn,n,j,k
m[i,i]→tp
0→fn
0→fp
0→n
For j,1,rowDim(m)
 © fn and fp start as the whole of row i and column i, tp included
 fn+m[i,j]→fn
 fp+m[j,i]→fp
 For k,1,colDim(m)
  n+m[j,k]→n
 EndFor
EndFor
fn-tp→fn
fp-tp→fp
n-tp-fn-fp→tn
Return {tp,fn,fp,tn}
EndFunc
